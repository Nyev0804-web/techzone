About Our TEA STATION

TEA STATION began as a small family kiosk in the hills, where our grandparents brewed tea for travelers passing by. Decades later, we still follow the same recipes, sourcing leaves directly from local farmers and roasting them in small batches.

Today we bring that tradition to your table — honest tea, carefully crafted, served with a warm smile.
